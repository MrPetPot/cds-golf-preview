What PRIMEandGOLF is, who stands behind it and how to reach us.

PRIMEandGOLF

An independent operation.

PRIMEandGOLF is a publicly accessible reference on the prime residential market tied to golf on the Costa del Sol: open, independent and kept up to date. It is built so that anyone can consult the figures, the method and comparable analysis without paywalls or registration.

Get in touch

A question, a correction, or a development we have missed.

Two routes to the same inbox: a general question about any aspect of the guide, or a submission from developers and agencies, which is checked against source.

Consulta general

A question about the guide

About the method, a figure, a course, a source or any other aspect of PRIMEandGOLF. We answer every enquiry; those that add something to the guide are incorporated, with their source.

We do not pass your data to third parties without your express permission. You may ask for it to be erased by writing to nicetomeetyou@primeandgolf.com.

For developers and agencies

Submit or correct information

If your development is missing, if a figure on its profile is wrong, or if you have better material than what is published, this is the route. It is checked against source and goes into the next edition.

Submitting information neither guarantees a place in the ranking nor changes a score: the criteria and their weightings are the same for every development.

Backed by

Malashpina Creativos

malashpina.es · @malashpinacreativos

A branding, communications and prime property marketing studio, with more than twenty years working alongside developers, funds and sales teams on the Costa del Sol. It edits and produces PRIMEandGOLF and is answerable for the verification of every figure. It plays no part in the criteria or the weightings: these are published in full and applied identically to every development. No one pays to appear.

Declared conflict of interest: the studio has worked with Concept Homes / NVOGA, the developer behind ZEW. ZEW is scored on the same formula as everything else, and it falls outside the top 10 in this edition.
