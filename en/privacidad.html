2 · What is collected, and what for

Only what you type into the forms on the contact page. There is no other point of collection.

  • Enquiry form: name, email, subject and your message. To answer whatever you ask about the guide.
  • Developer and agency form: company, contact name, email, telephone if you give one, reason, development and your message. To handle the submission or the correction of a figure on a profile.

Until a sending service is configured, the form transmits nothing by itself: it opens your email application with the message drafted, and it is you who decides whether to send it. Until that point the data does not leave your device.

3 · Legal basis

Your consent, given by ticking the box on the form, and the taking of pre-contractual steps where the enquiry requires it. Regulation (EU) 2016/679 and Spanish Organic Law 3/2018.

4 · Who else sees it

No one, unless you ask. If your enquiry asks us to put you in touch with the developer of a particular scheme, your details will have to be passed on, and that is done only with your express permission for that specific case. Your data is not sold, not rented and not added to any third-party list.

5 · How long it is kept

For as long as the relationship lasts, and afterwards for whatever period legal obligations require. If you ask for erasure, your data is deleted unless an obligation prevents it.

6 · Your rights

Access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction and portability. To exercise them, write to info@malashpina.es. If you believe your data has not been handled properly, you may complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD).

7 · Cookies and third parties

This guide installs no cookies and uses no local storage. It carries no analytics, no tracking pixels and no social media buttons. Verified against the published code.

It does load its typefaces from Google Fonts, which means your browser makes a request to Google's servers, and Google receives your IP address. That is the only third party involved, and it happens on every page.

The relationship map is drawn from our own coordinates, with no map tiles and no external services. Driving times come from measurements already taken, not from live queries.

8 · Security

Reasonable technical and organisational measures are applied to protect the data. The site is served over HTTPS.
